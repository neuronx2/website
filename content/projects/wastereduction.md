## Project Goal

To drive process efficiency and sustainability at Morgan Manufacturing by reducing material waste and water consumption through the application of Kaizen and Lean methodologies.

### Scope of Work

- Conducted root cause analysis of top waste-generating processes
- Led Kaizen events to identify quick wins and long-term fixes
- Designed and implemented water conservation protocols in operations
- Standardized improved processes for scalability across other production units

### Methodologies Used

- Kaizen workshops and operator-driven ideation
- 5 Whys and Fishbone diagram for root cause analysis
- PDCA cycles to validate and sustain improvements
- Standard Work to enforce best practices

### Stack

- Excel + SPC charts for waste tracking
- Kaizen tracker templates and impact scoring sheets
- MES system for process monitoring
- A3 problem solving templates

### Results

- Reduced raw material waste & proposed laternate use case
- Decreased water usage per output unit at plant level
- Improved frontline ownership of sustainability targets

### Strategic Impact

This project embedded continuous improvement into Morgan’s manufacturing DNA. By engaging teams through Kaizen and creating measurable gains in waste and water reduction, it demonstrated how sustainability and operational efficiency can be achieved simultaneously and scaled across sites.
