## Project Goal

To embed MMM outputs into the annual and quarterly marketing budget planning process, enabling data-driven decisions on channel allocation and spend efficiency. The objective was to connect past performance insights to future investment planning using scenario models and ROI-driven guidance.

### Stack

- Python + Statsmodels for ROI curve modeling and contribution analysis
- Excel + Tableau for budget planning tools and simulations
- SQL (Redshift) + dbt for spend and performance data processing
- MMM scenario templates and planning guides for cross-functional use

### Methodology

- Built ROI curves and saturation thresholds for each marketing channel
- Created Excel/Power BI tools for simulating channel mix and spend trade-offs
- Integrated MMM outcomes into CAC forecasts and LTV models
- Ran scenario workshops during QBRs and annual planning cycles
- Aligned recommendations with regional and global finance stakeholders

### Results

- Influenced global marketing budget through MMM-backed recommendations
- Reduced planning time by ~30% through use of ready-made simulation tools
- Increased stakeholder trust by providing transparent assumptions and ROI diagnostics
- Improved efficiency of budget allocation in underperforming and oversaturated channels

### Strategic Impact

This project positioned MMM not just as a measurement tool but as a strategic enabler. By embedding model insights directly into financial and marketing planning, the business achieved faster, smarter, and more defensible budgeting cycles across multiple regions.
