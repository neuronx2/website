## Customer Economics

I help organizations unlock the full economic potential of their customer base by combining behavioral analytics and financial modeling. My approach supports data-driven strategies across acquisition, retention, and monetization. I specialize in CRM and sales analytics, building conversion-optimized funnels, forecasting models, and real-time dashboards to align investments with measurable outcomes.

### 🔍 Key Highlights:
- Developed dynamic Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) models to forecast segment profitability.
- Conducted LTV:CAC ratio analyses to uncover acquisition inefficiencies and improve ROI.
- Segmented customers by behavior and revenue contribution to inform targeting strategies.
- Built dashboards to monitor churn, retention, and repeat purchase trends across cohorts.
- Applied economic metrics to optimize pricing, channel strategy, and loyalty programs.
- Designed CRM strategies aligned to lifecycle stages using behavioral and transactional data.
- Created automated dashboards tracking funnel performance and drop-off points in real time.
- Led A/B testing and cohort analysis to refine campaign effectiveness.
- Integrated CRM data across channels (email, paid, referral) into unified reporting for sales and marketing teams.
- Modeled CLV and CAC to inform strategic budgeting and personalized engagement tactics.
