## Fast moving, Data based business models

In the dynamic world of e-commerce and food tech, I focused on enhancing operational efficiency, customer retention, and personalized marketing strategies. Drawing inspiration from data-rich environments like **HelloFresh**, my work revolved around streamlining & optimizing customer acquisition and churn models, and building predictive insights for Revenue Management & Budgeting & Planning. With a strong emphasis on customer behavior analytics. The intersection of technology, user experience, and business agility defined my contributions, ensuring that both customer satisfaction and profitability grew hand-in-hand in a highly competitive market landscape.

### Key Focus Areas

- Customer Acquisition & Retention Analytics
- Predictive Customer Value Forecasting
- Churn Prediction & Loyalty Modeling
- Pricing and Discounting Strategy
- Customer Segmentation for Personalization Initiatives
- Marketing Attribution Modeling (Multi-touch)
- Cross-Sell and Up-Sell Strategy Development
