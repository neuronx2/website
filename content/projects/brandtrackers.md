## Project Goal

To design and execute a multi-country brand tracking study, enabling leadership to continuously monitor brand health, customer sentiment, and campaign effectiveness, while identifying early warning signals across markets.

## Project Overview

The objective was to deliver standardized, high-frequency insights across diverse markets to support brand management, marketing optimization, and competitive strategy.

## Stack

- Survey design tools for questionnaire development
- Fieldwork platforms for data collection across geographies
- SPSS and R for statistical analysis and validation
- Tableau and Power BI for dashboard delivery

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Tracked

- **Brand Awareness** (Unaided and Aided)
- **Brand Consideration**
- **Brand Preference**
- **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**
- **Brand Imagery Attributes**
- **Advertising Recall and Recognition**
- **Usage and Purchase Behavior**
- **Customer Satisfaction and Loyalty Metrics**

## Approach

- Stakeholder consultations to align on KPIs and local adaptations
- Development of globally standardized questionnaires
- Fieldwork management across multiple regions
- Data cleaning, statistical validation, and cross-market harmonization
- Real-time dashboard creation for actionable insights

## Results

- Delivered a unified brand tracking system across multiple countries
- Improved visibility into regional brand performance trends
- Enhanced ability to correlate marketing campaigns with brand outcomes
- Enabled faster, data-driven decision making for brand strategy
