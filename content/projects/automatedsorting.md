## Project Overview

As an independent consultant, I led the design and deployment of a fully automated, ML-powered fruit sorting and preservation system for HSAMB (a government agency) as part of a public-private partnership initiative. The project focused on modernizing post-harvest fruit handling by integrating smart technologies that reduce waste, improve quality control, and extend shelf life.

The system was designed to handle a wide range of fruits — including oranges, mandarins, lemons, and apples — and to process them with precision based on visual and chemical attributes.

### Scope of My Involvement
- **Technical Advisory & Procurement Support:** Acted as a strategic consultant to the client, helping them evaluate vendors and technologies aligned with project goals.  
- **Project Feasibility & Business Valuation:** Conducted a holistic evaluation of the project’s viability from financial, technical, and operational standpoints.  
- **System Integration Oversight:** Provided technical oversight to ensure smooth implementation and integration of subsystems across sorting, handling, and coating processes.  
- **Operations & Change Management:** Helped define workflows and operational protocols for daily use, training, and maintenance, ensuring a smooth transition to automation.  
- **Stakeholder Liaison:** Served as the bridge between the client and public sector stakeholders, managing expectations, reporting progress, and aligning deliverables with policy and commercial requirements.

### Key Technologies Implemented
- **Computer Vision & ML Algorithms:** Guided the client in selecting a system capable of sorting fruits based on color, size, surface defects, sweetness levels, and water content using machine learning and image processing.  
- **Robotic Arms & Conveyor Systems:** Evaluated available solutions for automated fruit handling to reduce labor dependency and increase operational efficiency.  
- **Washing & Edible Wax Coating Units:** Recommended post-harvest treatment systems that met food-grade safety standards while enhancing product shelf life and appearance.  
- **Modular, Scalable Architecture:** Advised on flexible infrastructure that could support a range of fruit types and scale with seasonal or regional demand.  
- **Sensor-Driven Quality Control:** Helped integrate IoT and sensor-based feedback systems for real-time performance monitoring and quality assurance.

### Project Outcomes
- Achieved high-accuracy sorting and consistent fruit quality across batches.  
- Significantly reduced post-harvest losses and manual processing costs.  
- Enhanced market readiness of produce through improved appearance and longer shelf life.  
- The success of the system has laid the groundwork for future scale-up and adoption across additional agricultural regions.
