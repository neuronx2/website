## Project Goal

To develop a robust and explainable attribution model that quantifies the true impact of each marketing channel on customer acquisition and retention. This model aimed to replace simplistic last-click models with advanced methods that reflect the complexity of multi-channel journeys in a food e-commerce context.

The ultimate goal was to empower performance marketers and finance teams to make informed, data-backed decisions regarding budget allocation, campaign design, and strategic growth initiatives.

### Stack

- Python (Data processing, modeling)
- SQL (Redshift) for data extraction from CRM, ad spend, and transactional systems
- dbt for modular and scalable transformation pipelines
- Power BI & Tableau for interactive dashboards and attribution visualizations
- Google Analytics + Meta Ads API for campaign metadata and spend metrics
- AWS Glue & S3 for orchestration and scalable data processing

### Methodology

- Unified user-level data across touchpoints using cookies, session IDs, and user IDs
- Evaluated legacy models (last-click, first-click, linear) to benchmark performance
- Implemented Markov Chain attribution to assess path probabilities and dropout risks
- Used Shapley value framework to fairly distribute conversion credit across channels
- Integrated cost and conversion data to assess ROI and channel efficiency
- Designed an experimentation framework to test incrementality of upper funnel channels (TV, Influencers, Podcasts)

### Results

- 15% reallocation of marketing budget to higher-impact channels
- Improved average conversion rate by 8% and reduced CPA by 2%
- Delivered ~$0.2M in annual campaign cost savings through optimised spend
- Built a continuously updated dashboard that became the single source of truth for campaign evaluation
- Strengthened cross-functional collaboration between CRM, Finance, and Marketing teams

### Strategic Impact

The attribution framework helped formalize quarterly marketing budget reviews and offered real-time feedback loops for channel managers. It became a critical tool for evaluating new campaigns, justifying spend increases, and guiding the transition to brand-awareness strategies in saturated markets. The data outputs also supported high-level presentations to the CMO and CFO for strategic planning and investor updates.
