## Project Goal

The objective of this project was to equip senior leadership with a robust, data-backed understanding of the competitive dynamics within the semiconductors and renewable energy sectors. The goal extended beyond just listing competitors — it involved uncovering strategic insights that would inform decisions around product innovation, pricing optimization, investment planning, and market expansion initiatives.

By integrating structured and unstructured data from a variety of sources — including patents, syndicated market research, import/export volumes, and competitor funding data — the project aimed to identify:

- Emerging trends and technology shifts influencing buyer behavior
- Gaps in the current product portfolio compared to competitors
- Opportunities for differentiation and strategic partnerships
- High-potential markets for organic or inorganic growth

The analysis was tailored to support expansion initiative and ultimately served as the foundation for building a continuous competitor intelligence function within the organization.

### Stack

- Python (scraping, clustering, sentiment analysis)
- Excel (financial benchmarking, price comparison)
- Patnav (patent analytics tool)
- Crunchbase, Pitchbook (company financials and funding analysis)
- Syndicated Research Reports (industry trends, projections)
- Import/Export Data Sources (for price, value, and volume analysis)

### Methodology

- Collected data from public sources: websites, job boards, datasheets, financial filings
- Applied SWOT, Porter's Five Forces, and Perceptual Mapping frameworks
- Built feature matrix and conducted clustering to identify whitespace opportunities
- Performed market share and pricing trend analysis

### Results

- Supported $275M expansion and M&A strategy
- Informed restructuring of product roadmap
- Developed internal competitor benchmarking blueprint
