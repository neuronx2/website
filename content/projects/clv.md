## Project Overview

In a fast-scaling (now listed) food-tech environment, I led the initiative to build a predictive Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) model. Acting as an in-house consultant and main analyist, my responsibilities spanned from strategic design to execution, ensuring the model addressed cross-functional needs across marketing, CRM, and finance departments. This model became the core of reporting in regards to measuring the efficiency of customer acquisition accross channels, regions, cohorts, products etc...

The project's primary goal was to predict customer value over 12 months, 3 years, and 5 years, enabling better cohort targeting, smarter retention investments, and improved revenue forecasting. 

### Approach

- **Model Development:** Built advanced cohort-based CLV prediction models using Python and Pandas. Integrated multiple behavioral, transactional, and demographic variables to improve predictive power.
- **Accuracy & Validation:** Achieved 88% predictive accuracy for the 12-month horizon. Long-term projections (3 and 5 years) were validated through historical backtesting techniques and on account of limited historical data these predictions were retained but rarely used.
- **Version Control and Deployment:** Implemented version-controlled pipelines for model retraining and monitoring, ensuring stability and adaptability as customer behavior evolved.
- **Strategic Embedding:** Collaborated with senior leadership to embed model outputs into business decision processes, including budgeting and campaign strategy.

### Stack

- Python (Pandas, Scikit-learn)
- SQL for data extraction and transformations
- Tableau for interactive CLV dashboards
- Git for version control

### Results

- Improved customer segmentation and targeting.
- Retention campaign ROI increased by ~8%.
- Provided leadership with reliable, forward-looking revenue forecasts, enhancing strategic planning.
