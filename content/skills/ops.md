## Operational Excellence & Continuous Improvement

I help organizations unlock efficiency, reduce waste, and sustain high performance through data-driven process improvements. My work is rooted in Lean Six Sigma, Kaizen, and process benchmarking—deployed across digital, operational, and service functions. Whether optimizing TATs, reducing overhead, or scaling delivery, I’ve driven measurable improvements through a balance of structure and agility.

- Led process optimization initiatives using Lean Six Sigma and Kaizen across product, marketing, and operations teams.
- Reduced turnaround time (TAT) and increased resource utilization through targeted capacity planning frameworks.
- Used data analysis and visual RCA tools to identify systemic inefficiencies and track root cause patterns.
- Designed operational dashboards to monitor KPIs in real time and support proactive decision-making.
- Integrated continuous improvement practices into strategic planning, team workflows, and stakeholder reviews.
