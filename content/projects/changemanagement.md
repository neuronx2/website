## Project Overview

In a fast-growing IT-enabled marketing research firm, I spearheaded a change management project centered around improving operational performance through KPI transparency and team-driven accountability.

Faced with inconsistent project delivery timelines and fluctuating service quality, leadership recognized the need for a structured performance improvement initiative. Inspired by McKinsey's influence model for successful change management, the project sought to embed a "performance-first" culture across all operational teams.

## Approach

- **Identified Core Operational KPIs:**  
  Collaborated with department heads to define critical metrics for each team:
  - Project Turnaround Time
  - Client Feedback Scores
  - Internal SLA Adherence
  - Utilization Rates

- **KPI Dashboards by Team:**  
  Designed visual, accessible dashboards updated weekly, with trends, benchmarks, and actionable commentary.

- **Transparent Communication:**  
  Rolled out KPI visibility across the organization, ensuring every team member understood the key levers of performance.

- **Performance Reviews and Celebrations:**  
  Integrated KPI progress into monthly team meetings. High-performing teams received internal recognition and leadership praise.

- **Empowerment through Training:**  
  Provided targeted workshops on data interpretation and operational best practices to help teams translate insights into actions.

- **Cascading Leadership Alignment:**  
  Weekly leadership reviews were streamlined around KPIs, enhancing focus and accelerating support for resource bottlenecks.

## Results

- Increased Accountability:  
  Teams took greater ownership of outcomes, leading to a 22% reduction in project turnaround time within six months.

- Cultural Shift:  
  KPI discussions shifted from defensive posturing to proactive problem-solving.

- Sustainable Improvement:  
  Departments continued updating and evolving KPIs as business complexity grew, demonstrating ownership of performance management.

- Client Satisfaction:  
  External client satisfaction scores improved by 18% over the project period.

## Key Takeaways

The project reinforced critical change management fundamentals:
- Role modeling desired behaviors (leadership buy-in).
- Fostering understanding and conviction.
- Developing team capabilities to deliver.
- Reinforcing behaviors through processes (KPI-based reviews).

This structured, transparent approach to managing change was pivotal in strengthening operational resilience and agility.
