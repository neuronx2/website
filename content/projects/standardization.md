## Project Goal

To institutionalize repeatable, scalable workflows across multiple high-impact business domains—ranging from research delivery and quoting to CRM, M&A, and financial scenario modeling. These efforts eliminated ambiguity, accelerated delivery, and created the foundation for automation and cross-market scalability.

### Scope of Work

- Developed SOPs and toolkits for quoting, CRM usage, M&A tracking, and survey fielding
- Built templates and reusable dashboards for finance, strategy, and marketing functions
- Standardized stakeholder inputs, assumptions, and documentation protocols across teams
- Trained and onboarded teams into the new ways of working

### Standardized Areas

- Research project delivery workflows across 600+ surveys (sampling, QC, feasibility)
- Lean/Six Sigma-based quoting and bid cycle optimization
- CRM architecture with structured field definitions and automated flows
- MMM-based scenario planning and budget modeling
- M&A tracker design with reusable scoring matrices and due diligence workflows

### Stack

- Excel + Python + VBA for logic modeling and automation
- Power BI + Tableau for visualizing process output and monitoring SLA adherence
- dbt + SQL for unified data definitions and modeling pipelines
- Confluence / Notion for SOP documentation
- CRM, Jira, and internal PM tools for execution standardization

### Results

- Reduced project turnaround times by 30–50% across quoting and planning processes
- Increased process repeatability across functions (research, finance, corp dev)
- Lowered onboarding ramp-up through consistent documentation and templates
- Enabled smooth collaboration and cross-department alignment on shared workflows

### Strategic Impact

This project transformed previously ad hoc or founder-driven processes into enterprise-ready, documented systems. It enabled faster, more confident execution and laid the groundwork for process automation, cross-market replication, and operational excellence across several business units.
