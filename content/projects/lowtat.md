## Project Goal

To streamline the project bidding and proposal generation process in a fast-paced marketing research environment. The objective was to reduce turnaround time (TAT), eliminate redundant steps, and improve accuracy of scoping through Lean and Six Sigma methodologies, enabling faster client response without compromising feasibility or profitability.

### Stack

- Lean Six Sigma (DMAIC) for structured process redesign
- Excel + VBA for quote automation and pricing tools
- Power BI for TAT monitoring and SLA adherence
- Jira + Smartsheet for handoff coordination
- Salesforce integration for intake-to-scope workflow

### Methodology

- Mapped current state process across Sales, Pricing, and Delivery
- Identified bottlenecks using Pareto analysis and VOC sessions
- Defined CTQs and restructured quoting workflow using DMAIC
- Built decision trees and quote templates to standardize effort estimation
- Created dashboards and SLA trackers to sustain improvements

### Results

- Reduced average bidding TAT by 36%
- Increased proposal win rate by 14% due to faster turnaround
- Cut rework and clarification cycles by 40% using FTR tracking
- Increased inter-team transparency and role clarity

### Strategic Impact

The project transformed a fragmented and manual quoting process into a streamlined, scalable workflow. It applied Lean Six Sigma in a knowledge-services setting and established operational discipline in a high-variance, high-pressure business environment. The success laid the foundation for quoting process replication across global delivery centers.
