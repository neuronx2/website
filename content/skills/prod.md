## New Product Development & Innovation

I bring analytical structure to the innovation process—bridging customer needs, business strategy, and technical feasibility. Whether defining R&D pipelines or building idea-to-launch roadmaps, I’ve helped teams reduce time-to-market and increase ROI. My work spans product blueprinting, innovation funnel design, and VoC-driven prioritization, with measurable impact across tech, manufacturing, and consumer goods sectors.

### 🔍 Key Highlights:
- Created standardized blueprinting frameworks to align market needs with engineering and go-to-market teams.
- Applied Voice of Customer (VoC) insights to shape product roadmaps and feature sets.
- Reduced idea-to-launch cycles by optimizing stage-gate processes across innovation pipelines.
- Developed Digial products like analytics dashboards, prediction models for tracking performance, product adoption, and ROI.
- Led feasibility studies and market-entry assessments for multiple product launches and vertical expansions.
