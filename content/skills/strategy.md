## Data-Backed Expansion Strategy Development

I design expansion strategies rooted in data, not just assumptions. From new market entry to M&A planning and organizational scaling, my approach combines financial modeling, competitive analysis, and go-to-market insight to unlock sustainable growth. I’ve partnered with leadership teams to evaluate strategic investments, size market opportunities, and align expansion plans with operational realities.

### 🔍 Key Highlights:
- Developed data-driven frameworks for market sizing, location prioritization, and audience expansion.
- Led M&A feasibility analysis, synergy tracking, and integration planning across multiple geographies.
- Modeled expansion scenarios tied to TAM/SAM/SOM metrics and financial/operational performance targets.
- Built executive dashboards to support strategic decision-making across funding, hiring, and GTM rollout.
- Worked directly with CXOs and VPs to align expansion goals with infrastructure, compliance, and budget requirements.
