## Project Overview

At SBI Credit Cards — one of India's largest credit card issuers — I was part of the finance analytics division before being repositioned to lead collections operations during the 2008 global financial crisis. Tasked with managing a large geographic region, I directed field officers, agency partners, and internal recovery teams.

The project aimed to navigate the surge in bad debts following the mortgage crash, stabilize recovery rates, and ensure compliance with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) norms on non-performing assets.  
Operational scale was immense, requiring quick adaptation, decisive field strategies, and strong vendor management under highly challenging conditions.

## Key Contributions

- **Data-Driven Segmentation**:
  - Conducted **classic data analysis** on delinquency patterns, customer repayment history, and transaction behavior to categorize accounts.
  - Segregated accounts into genuine hardship cases, intentional defaulters, fraud-prone accounts, and disputes.

- **Targeted Recovery Strategies**:
  - Allocated field resources and dunning actions based on segment prioritization.
  - Focused legal and escalated actions primarily on high-value intentional defaulters, maximizing recovery efficiency.

- **Controlled Legal Actions**:
  - Introduced a selective legal action model to minimize costs on low-potential accounts.
  - Ensured legal compliance while maintaining an aggressive stance on recoverable portfolios.

- **Field Operations Management**:
  - Led a wide geographic area with on-ground field officers and vendor partners.
  - Implemented daily tracking dashboards for agencies, improving transparency, agent productivity, and compliance.

- **Legal Expertise Integration**:
  - After completing a law qualification, embedded regulatory insights into collections strategy.
  - Developed a small legal collections portfolio based on applicable debt recovery laws.

- **Dispute Resolution Optimization**:
  - Created protocols to fast-track genuine disputes and resolve customer issues without unnecessary escalations.

## Challenges Addressed

- Rising delinquency and bad debt during extreme financial turbulence.
- Cost management under severe operational and regulatory pressures.
- Managing agency partners across vast geographic dispersion while ensuring consistent execution quality.

## Impact

- **Consistent achievement and over-achievement of monthly and quarterly recovery targets**.
- **Over 20% reduction in collections-related legal costs** by smarter targeting.
- **Enhanced field force productivity** through improved segmentation and data visibility.
- **Strengthened compliance and reputational risk management** during a highly sensitive period for financial institutions.
