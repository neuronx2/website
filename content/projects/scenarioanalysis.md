## Project Goal

To design and deploy scenario analysis frameworks across a wide range of business decisions—from financial restructuring and inorganic expansion to marketing budget planning and campaign strategy. This cross-functional capability enabled leadership teams to simulate outcomes, assess risks, and make confident decisions under uncertainty.

### Scope of Work

- Developed models to simulate business outcomes under different strategic assumptions
- Used for financial restructuring, campaign planning, pricing optimization, and geographic expansion
- Enabled cross-functional planning for marketing, finance, and strategy teams
- Built scenario templates for use in board meetings, QBRs, and operational reviews

### Frameworks & Methodologies Used

- 3-Case Financial Modeling: Base, Optimistic, Conservative
- Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM) for ROI-based spend reallocation
- M&A valuation banding and synergy impact projections
- Recovery modeling under SARFAESI and DRT frameworks
- Scenario matrices for expansion strategies (greenfield, partnership, M&A)
- ROI curve forecasting and saturation modeling for performance channels

### Stack

- Excel + Python for simulation and forecasting logic
- Power BI and Tableau for dashboard-driven scenarios
- SQL + dbt for data pipelines supporting modeling inputs
- Pitchbook, Crunchbase, CRM tools for external parameter benchmarking

### Use Cases

- Financial restructuring plan simulations under legal supervision (SARFAESI/DRT)
- Discount and voucher efficiency optimization using ROI forecasting
- Budget reallocation across TV, digital, and influencer channels via MMM
- Inorganic growth analysis comparing market-entry modes and risk profiles
- Turnaround TAT modeling in marketing research bid cycles

### Results

- Informed ~$400M in cross-functional strategic decisions
- Accelerated planning cycles with standardized scenario templates
- Enabled QBR- and board-level planning with actionable forecasts
- Increased confidence and clarity in decision-making for CFOs, CMOs, and CEOs

### Strategic Impact

This work established scenario modeling as a foundational business planning capability across departments. It drove alignment, accelerated decision cycles, and ensured investments and restructuring plans were grounded in data and multiple forward-looking views.
