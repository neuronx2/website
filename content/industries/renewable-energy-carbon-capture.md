## Renewable Energy & Carbon Capture

My work in the renewable energy and carbon capture domain has focused on enabling smart, sustainable decisions through data. Whether helping firms model the financial viability of wind or solar energy investments, I’ve applied analytics to identify the emergin new technologiy and companies.

In collaboration with project teams, Marketing Research or through independent consulting, I’ve explored the applications of high-performance materials—like charge transfer, thermal insulation, fire protection systems etc.components—used in renewable energy systemsand brining them to R&D team to develop & reserach new products at **Morgan Advanced Materials**.

With organic & inorganic expansion in focus, using detailed Patent info analysis and Startup Funding information, I’ve also assessed potential carbon reduction technologies that can help shape the future.

### Key Focus Areas

- Scenario-based modeling for renewable investments  
- Optimization of energy storage and battery systems    
- Risk-adjusted ROI analysis for decarbonization projects    
- Tracking and validating carbon offset strategies  
- Identification of future tech trends based on Patent & Trade Analysis
- Material tech used in carbon capture
- Green Hydrogen & Renewable energy
