## Project Goal

To execute and deliver high-quality, high-volume quantitative research projects for a global base of clients. Over several years, I supported more than 600 projects involving questionnaire consulting, sampling strategy, data quality assurance, and insight readiness—ensuring efficient and compliant delivery across diverse research needs.

### Scope of Work

- Reviewed and optimized survey instruments to improve respondent engagement and data clarity
- Advised on sampling feasibility, targeting, and quota balancing
- Conducted data validation, straightlining checks, and open-end QC
- Delivered summary insights and client-ready files in required formats
- Supported B2B and B2C verticals, from concept testing to ad evaluation and brand trackers

### Stack

- SPSS for data cleaning and statistical QC
- Excel for quotas, tracking sheets, weights, and deliverables
- Survey platforms: Confirmit, Decipher, Qualtrics
- PowerPoint + Google Slides for insight reporting
- Internal PM systems for quote tracking, delivery SLAs, and documentation

### Results

- Delivered 600+ research projects with >98% satisfaction score
- Improved survey efficiency and response quality across hundreds of instruments
- Supported multimarket project coordination with translation QA and regional feasibility checks
- Reduced fieldwork re-runs through rigorous pre-launch review processes
- Mentored junior team members and codified SOPs for global delivery teams

### Strategic Impact

This work established a foundation of trust and reliability with both clients and internal teams. My ability to balance technical data standards with practical client needs ensured actionable outcomes from fast-paced research environments. These contributions also helped scale consistent delivery practices across geographies and project types.
