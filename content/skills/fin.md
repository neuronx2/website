## Financial Modeling & Scenario Analysis

I design financial models that support clarity, agility, and strategic foresight. My work spans from operational budgeting and liquidity forecasting to investment planning and scenario modeling—often in collaboration with CFOs, senior leadership, and strategy teams. These models have informed decisions around expansion, resource allocation, and financial restructuring across startups, corporates, and public-private partnerships.

### 🔍 Key Highlights:
- Created end-to-end models for budgeting, cash flow forecasting, and project viability across multiple sectors.
- Designed scenario-planning templates used for strategic decision-making, including new market entry and M&A feasibility.
- Collaborated with C-suite executives to align financial metrics with operational and growth strategies.
- Built models using tools like Excel, Python, and MS Dynamics, incorporating dynamic inputs and flexible outputs.
- Supported turnaround and restructuring efforts through risk-adjusted modeling and cost-benefit frameworks.
