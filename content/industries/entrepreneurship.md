## Overview

Working across entrepreneurial ventures and small-scale projects has been one of the most dynamic aspects of my professional journey. I have contributed to initiatives ranging from recycling, refurbishing to food processing, where agility and multidisciplinary problem-solving were critical.

This experience allowed me to wear multiple hats — spanning finance, marketing, data analytics, and operations — enabling me to design lean strategies, optimize customer acquisition, build financial models, and ensure diversification, expansion and management of various small businesses. Whether managing day-to-day operations or aligning digital initiatives with business goals, every project sharpened my ability to drive impactful outcomes with limited resources.

### Key Focus Areas

- Customer acquisition and digital marketing
- Retail store Site selection and marketing
- Financial modeling and cash flow planning
- Data-driven performance management
- Market research and competitor analysis
- Growth strategy for early-stage ventures
- Analytics for business insights
- Project Bidding & Scope Identification
- Liaison and relationship management
