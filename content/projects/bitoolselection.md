## Project Overview

In 2016, facing rapid data growth and fragmented reporting processes, we initiated a project to evaluate and implement a modern Business Intelligence (BI) solution. The organization’s legacy setup relied heavily on Excel cubes linked to MongoDB databases, creating performance bottlenecks, governance challenges, and reporting inefficiencies.

The goal was to recommend a BI platform that could centralize data access, streamline reporting, and establish a foundation for scalable, governed analytics practices.

## Business Case

The existing reporting landscape was fragmented:

- Multiple Excel files with heavy data cubes linked to live databases.
- Siloed data sources causing inconsistencies and versioning issues.
- Absence of standardized reporting pipelines.
- Growing demand for real-time and self-service analytics.

The leadership recognized that without a scalable BI infrastructure, growth would exacerbate operational inefficiencies, decision delays, and data mistrust.

The project focused on selecting a tool that could:
- Seamlessly integrate with Excel, MongoDB, and future cloud sources.
- Reduce dependency on manual data preparation.
- Enforce governance principles like version control, access security, and data lineage.

## Approach

- **Tool Evaluation**: Conducted a comparative assessment of Power BI, Tableau, and Qlik, scoring each against scalability, integration ease, governance capabilities, visualization quality, and total cost of ownership (TCO).
- **Data Architecture Analysis**: Mapped the existing Excel/MongoDB ecosystem and proposed streamlined pipelines.
- **Governance Framework Development**: Drafted an initial governance blueprint including data stewardship roles, source cataloging, and dashboard certification processes.

## Stack & Activities

- **Power BI**: Selected for its flexibility, Microsoft ecosystem compatibility, strong governance integrations (Row Level Security, Auditing), and cost-efficiency.
- **Data Integration**: Connected MongoDB collections and Excel sources into unified Power BI datasets.
- **Governance Principles**: Established basic guidelines for data owners, certifiers, and end-user reporting responsibilities.

## Results

- Recommended **Power BI** deployment with clear roadmap and governance policies.
- Reporting turnaround time reduced by **20%** within the first six months.
- Improved data reliability and established a scalable foundation for self-service analytics.
- Set the stage for future cloud migration and advanced analytics projects.
