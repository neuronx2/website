## Project Goal

To build a robust digital backbone for cross-functional collaboration at a manufacturing company facing coordination challenges between product, marketing, finance, and engineering teams.

### Stack

- Sharepoint for async communication and knowledge sharing
- Jira + Confluence for sprint planning and delivery documentation
- Segment + Amplitude for unified product analytics

### Implementation Steps

- Conducted process mapping to identify handoff issues across departments
- Introduced shared rituals and communication standards (demo days, retros, QBRs)
- Unified feedback cycles using collaborative tooling (Figma → Jira, Sharepoint pages)
- Built integrations between project management tools and data pipelines
- Standardized reporting templates to align OKRs and KPIs across teams

### Results

- Improved OKR alignment and visibility through shared dashboards
- Increased stakeholder engagement with ~40% higher participation in planning cycles
- Decreased tool sprawl and redundant reporting overhead

### Strategic Impact

This initiative fostered a culture of transparency and shared ownership at scale. By digitalising collaboration, the firm unlocked higher productivity, faster release cycles, and improved product-market iteration velocity.
