## Market Research & Consultancy

My work in market research spans both traditional and agile methodologies, enabling data-backed decision-making across product development, brand positioning, and customer experience strategy. I've collaborated on projects involving large-scale quantitative surveys, segmentation modeling, and advanced analytics derived from panel-based data — often in fast-paced consumer-focused sectors. Using tools like conjoint analysis, max-diff, and text analytics, I've supported projects aimed at uncovering latent customer needs, evaluating product-market fit, and identifying key value drivers. I’ve also designed and optimized survey flows, collaborated with panel providers such as **Dynata and Toluna**, and developed reporting solutions that merge attitudinal and behavioral datasets to inform go-to-market and retention strategies.

Apart from delivering Marketing research Projects, I have also worked as a Finance and Planning Analyst, closely with C-Level executives in this industry.

### Key Focus Areas

- Survey Design, Sampling & Flow Optimization  
- Conjoint Analysis & Max-Diff (Trade-off Modeling)  
- Brand Awareness & Tracking Studies     
- Text Mining & NLP on Open-Ended Responses  
- Retention, Loyalty & Voice of Customer Analytics
- Data Visualization & Automated Reporting Dashboards
- Indian GAAP, IFRS & Compliance
- Financial Planning & Analysis (MS Dynamics, Cash Flow Analysis)
