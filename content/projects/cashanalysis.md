## Project Overview

As part of an IT-enabled marketing research startup experiencing rapid growth across multiple international markets, I was responsible for designing and implementing a cash flow analysis framework to proactively support the leadership team's financial planning and operational decisions.

The core objective was to create dynamic, scenario-driven cash flow models that would:

- Provide clear visibility into operational cash needs.
- Strengthen financial controls around client billing cycles and vendor payments.
- Enable strategic discussions around growth investments and funding milestones.

## Approach

- **Historical Data Consolidation:**  
  Mapped all sources of revenue (client retainers, project-based payments) and major cash outflows (staffing, technology investments, marketing spend) across the previous 12 months.

- **Rolling Cash Flow Forecast:**  
  Built a dynamic 13-week rolling forecast model, updated weekly, enabling the executive team to view cash position changes in real-time.

- **Scenario Analysis:**  
  Simulated multiple growth and expenditure scenarios — including aggressive market expansion, conservative growth, and operational contingencies.

- **Key Financial KPIs:**  
  Introduced and monitored key liquidity indicators to support leadership in decision-making:
  - Monthly Burn Rate
  - Cash Runway (Months of Operations Funded)
  - Accounts Receivable Turnover
  - Net Cash Conversion Cycle

- **Reporting and Alignment:**  
  Delivered tailored, executive-level reports highlighting forecasted cash positions, critical inflection points, and recommendations for managing reserves or accelerating collections.

## Results

- Improved Strategic Visibility:  
  Leadership gained 6+ months of proactive cash flow visibility, enabling better timing of expansion initiatives and financing discussions.

- Operational Efficiency:  
  Optimized vendor payment terms and client collections, improving working capital turnover.

- Strengthened Investor Confidence:  
  Detailed cash flow forecasts and scenario plans were instrumental during strategic investor meetings and subsequent fundraising rounds.
