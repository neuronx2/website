## Project Goal

To design and execute a comprehensive expansion strategy for entering new markets and unlocking new revenue streams. This included both organic (capacity and geographic growth) and inorganic (M&A) avenues across industrial manufacturing and e-commerce sectors. The objective was to integrate data-driven insight into every phase — from market identification to post-expansion operational planning.

### Stack

- SQL + Excel: Market modeling, unit economics, ROI simulation  
- Python: Customer segmentation, clustering, demand forecasting  
- Power BI: Executive-level dashboards and expansion playbooks  
- Pitchbook + Crunchbase: M&A scouting and screening  
- SAP + MS Dynamics: Financial data consolidation and cost simulation  
- Azure Data Lake: Storage for unified multi-country, multi-SBU datasets  

### Methodology

- Conducted TAM/SAM/SOM analysis for emerging regions and verticals  
- Built an M&A screening engine to shortlist and compare 20+ potential acquisition targets  
- Designed market readiness scoring based on competition, regulations, and supply chain factors  
- Simulated greenfield, brownfield, and partnership-led entry using cost-benefit models  
- Delivered "Idea-to-Market" workflow analysis to speed up innovation cycles  

### Frameworks Used

- SWOT and Porter’s Five Forces for competitive assessment  
- Ansoff Matrix to define expansion modes (market penetration, diversification)  
- Blue Ocean Strategy to find uncontested market space  
- Financial scenario modeling using 3-case structure (Base, Optimistic, Conservative)  
- MoSCoW prioritization for investment initiatives  
- M&A Playbook: Strategic fit, synergy mapping, and integration readiness checklist  

### Results

- Lead global expansion strategy valued at over $275M in both industrial and B2C sectors
- Convinced board to approve $75m organic project for capacity expansion  
- Reduced time-to-decision by using live dashboards and decision-ready models   
- Identified and shaped $75M in organic capacity expansion and production realignment  
- Drove strategic partner alliances including academic R&D (e.g., MoU with Penn State)  

### Strategic Impact

The project redefined how expansion strategy was approached — replacing fragmented intuition-led decisions with structured, data-backed planning. It empowered the leadership team with foresight and flexibility, building a playbook that became a repeatable model across new geographies, verticals, and business units. The integrated use of strategy, analytics, and stakeholder governance led to sustainable, scalable growth.
