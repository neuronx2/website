## Project Goal

To review, edit, and enhance an investor education book designed to improve financial literacy and promote responsible investing among Indian retail investors. The content aligned with NISM standards and was deployed through SEBI-supported public campaigns.

### Scope of Work

- Edited and structured chapters on equity, derivatives, commodities, and mutual funds
- Simplified technical finance content for accessibility across Tier 2/3 cities
- Ensured consistency with SEBI, RBI, NSE/BSE regulatory language
- Reviewed examples, terminology, and risk disclosures for clarity and compliance
- Collaborated with the author (professor) and publisher to finalize educational modules

### Topics Covered

- Introduction to financial markets and investment goals
- Equity markets, order types, indices, and investor behavior
- Futures and options: payoff mechanics, hedging, leverage
- Commodities: contracts, physical vs cash settlement
- Mutual fund types, SIP strategy, NAV interpretation
- Risk profiling and portfolio diversification techniques
- Investor grievance redressal, rights, and governance

### Results

- Book adopted in SEBI-backed investor awareness sessions nationwide
- Used by partner NGOs and training institutes across India
- Improved accessibility of financial concepts for first-time investors
- Strengthened compliance and credibility of the education provider
- Supported national goal of increasing financial literacy and inclusion

### Strategic Impact

This project helped bridge the knowledge gap in India’s growing investor base by converting complex capital market instruments into accessible educational material. It highlighted the importance of financial literacy in enabling safer investment decisions and reinforced the country’s push for inclusive financial participation.
