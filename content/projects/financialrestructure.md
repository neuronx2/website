## Project Goal

To design and implement a comprehensive financial restructuring plan for a family-run SME that had defaulted on its loan obligations and faced enforcement under India’s SARFAESI Act and DRT Act. The aim was to realign the business towards financial sustainability by negotiating terms with creditors, securing legal protection, and developing a feasible revival strategy aligned with RBI norms.

### Stack

- Excel + SQL for financial forecasting, cash flow modeling, and debt service schedules  
- Legal frameworks: SARFAESI Act (2002), DRT Act, RBI restructuring guidelines, MSME rehabilitation policies  
- Scenario analysis tools: 3-statement forecasting models with restructuring overlays  
- Documentation: Revival proposal preparation, affidavit formatting, compliance reporting  
- Stakeholder engagement: Bank officers, ARCs, valuers, and legal representatives

### Methodology

- Diagnosed financial health and cash flow gaps using past performance and forward projections  
- Mapped collateral coverage and lender exposure to support negotiation strategy  
- Developed a revival plan including equity infusion, repayment moratorium, and debt realignment   
- Facilitated due diligence, field visits, and third-party asset evaluations

### Results

- Achieved formal debt restructuring and deferred loan repayments by ~18 months  
- Reduced monthly debt obligations by over 20% through tenure extension and interest recalibration  
- Prevented asset auction and protected operational continuity  
- Restored business creditworthiness and enabled requalification for government projects  
- Maintained compliance with all RBI and legal requirements throughout the restructuring process

### Strategic Impact

This project showcased how financially distressed SMEs can be stabilized through a well-coordinated blend of financial planning, legal compliance, and stakeholder alignment. The solution preserved the value of underlying assets, restored banking relationships, and laid a foundation for long-term viability.
