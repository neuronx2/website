## Project Goal

To contribute to the development of a DNA vaccine for prostate cancer in collaboration with Dr. G.P. Talwar, a leading Indian immunologist. The objective was to produce and validate immunogenic DNA constructs, test protein expression, and support preclinical vaccine development through precise molecular and cell biology techniques.

### Scope of Work

- Performed DNA extraction, PCR amplification, and gene cloning
- Conducted in vitro protein synthesis and verified expression using SDS-PAGE and Western blot
- Maintained mammalian cell lines for tissue culture experiments
- Assisted in plasmid purification and vaccine candidate preparation

### Techniques Used

- DNA extraction, Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR)
- Protein synthesis, SDS-PAGE, Western blotting, ELISA
- Tissue culture and transfection of prostate cancer cell lines (e.g., LNCaP, PC-3)
- Vaccine extraction and immunogen validation

### Results

- Contributed to a working DNA vaccine prototype targeting prostate cancer
- Validated antigenic protein expression and sequence accuracy for candidate immunogens
- Supported vaccine preclinical development for further animal trials
- Strengthened workflows for antigen testing and gene delivery optimization

### Strategic Impact

This work contributed towards therapeutic concept—India’s own DNA-based vaccine platform targeting prostate cancer. The role demanded cross-functional expertise in wet-lab protocols, attention to scientific rigor, and alignment with translational research goals for indigenous immunotherapy. Clinical trials for this vaccine received approval from the Drugs Controller General of India, but were interrupted due to the COVID-19 pandemic.
