## Project Goal

To implement a robust CRM system tailored to the operational and marketing needs of a B2C internet-based service aggregator. The project focused on selecting the right CRM tool and embedding scalable data governance practices from the beginning to ensure seamless automation, accurate tracking, and compliance as the business scaled.

### Stack

- HubSpot CRM for sales, marketing, and customer lifecycle tracking
- Zapier + Make (Integromat) for third-party automation
- Segment + Google Tag Manager (GTM) for behavioral event tracking
- Google Sheets + BigQuery for data QA and backup
- Calendly + Google Workspace for service coordination

### Implementation Steps

- Conducted workflow audit and CRM platform evaluation (HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce, Pipedrive)
- Selected HubSpot based on flexibility and startup-friendly features
- Defined custom fields, user journeys, and lifecycle stages in CRM
- Created role-based access and structured audit trails
- Integrated website forms, CTAs, and service bookings into CRM pipeline
- Implemented automated workflows for follow-ups, upselling, and feedback collection
- Designed dashboards to monitor conversion rates, booking quality, and funnel health

### Data Governance Practices

- Established clear data definitions and field documentation
- Designed governance framework for data access, version control, and auditability
- Enforced opt-in mechanisms and GDPR-compliant data handling
- Built automation logs and data integrity checks

### Results

- Digitized full lead-to-service lifecycle with 100% CRM integration
- Improved lead conversion speed through automation
- Increased service completion and satisfaction rates through structured follow-ups
- Enabled category and region-wise performance analysis
- Positioned CRM as the backbone of growth and cross-functional planning

### Strategic Impact

The project built a future-proof CRM foundation that allowed the platform to expand service offerings and geographical reach with minimal friction. Governance-first design ensured long-term data health and reporting reliability. The CRM system empowered both marketing and operations teams to collaborate effectively using a single source of truth.
