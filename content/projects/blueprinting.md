## Project Overview

Morgan Advanced Materials sought to accelerate the time-to-market for new graphite-based products across sectors like renewable energy, semiconductors, and advanced manufacturing. Traditional development timelines were slow due to legacy internal processes. To drive innovation, a comprehensive New Product Blueprinting and Stage-Gate framework was implemented.

## Business Case

The need for faster innovation stemmed from competitive pressure and emerging market opportunities. Graphite products, critical for energy storage and semiconductor applications, required quicker prototyping and customer validation. Aligning R&D, product management, and commercial teams around a standardized, customer-centric approach became essential.

## Approach

- **New Product Blueprinting**: Introduced systematic customer interviews to map real unmet needs early in the innovation cycle. Emphasis was placed on gathering precise, voice-of-customer (VOC) insights before heavy investments.
- **Stage-Gate Process Enhancement**: Redesigned the existing stage-gate process to create more flexible and responsive development cycles. Key updates included:
  - Earlier concept validation gates
  - Clearer Go/Kill criteria tied to market feedback
  - Agile iteration loops after initial prototyping
- **Cross-Functional Collaboration**: Created joint ownership between engineering, commercial, and operations teams to maintain momentum across stages.
- **Tool Enablement**: Implemented shared documentation, templates, and tracking systems for transparency across all teams.

## Key Steps in Implementation

1. Conduct VOC research using Blueprinting frameworks (Discovery Interviews, Preference Interviews)
2. Define commercial and technical success metrics collaboratively
3. Rapid concept prototyping based on top customer needs
4. Accelerated initial stage-gate reviews based on minimum viable product (MVP) outputs
5. Full stage-gate with customer pilot trials before full-scale investment

## Results

- 30% reduction in idea-to-launch cycle time
- Improved first-year commercial success rates of new products by 20%
- Stronger alignment between R&D efforts and commercial market needs
