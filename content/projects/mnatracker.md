## Project Goal

To design and implement a comprehensive M&A tracking system that supported executive leadership in identifying, evaluating, and prioritizing inorganic growth opportunities. The tracker enabled real-time visibility of deal pipelines, integrated scoring models, and scenario planning for expansion into new markets and product lines.

### Stack

- Excel + Power Query for structured data analysis and pipeline updates
- Power BI + Sharepoint for interactive dashboards and risk-return matrices
- Pitchbook + Crunchbase APIs for live company data and deal flow
- M&A Playbook: templates for diligence, valuation, and risk profiling

### Methodology

- Structured a multi-tier M&A pipeline segmented by sector, region, and strategic theme
- Developed scoring models using weighted KPIs like strategic fit, IP value, integration risk, and financial health
- Created dashboards to compare valuation bands, EBITDA multiples, and deal stages
- Integrated legal, tax, and operational readiness markers into the tracker
- Enabled real-time collaboration across Strategy, Finance, Legal, and Product teams

### Results

- Evaluated 200+ M&A targets over 12 months across 8 geographies
- Identified and prioritized 30 high-fit opportunities for deep diligence
- Accelerated initial screening cycle by 80% through standardized workflows
- Tracker adopted as the core tool by the corporate development team

### Strategic Impact

The project institutionalized a scalable M&A pipeline management approach, replacing fragmented spreadsheets with a unified, insight-rich platform. It enabled disciplined decision-making, facilitated cross-functional alignment, and became a cornerstone of long-term inorganic growth planning.
