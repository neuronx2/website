## Project Overview

In 2010, I successfully led a turnkey warehousing project bid under the Haryana State Agricultural Development and Welfare Corporation (HAFED)'s Private Entrepreneur Godown (PEG) scheme. As a consultant, I managed end-to-end project delivery: from identifying the opportunity to handing over a fully operational 88,000 metric ton storage facility. 

The project involved extensive cross-functional expertise: regulatory compliance, financial modeling, land procurement, infrastructure planning, legal documentation, and public sector liaison. A key differentiator was using a nuanced understanding of the “Renting + Preservation” clause, enabling my client to bid higher rental rates yet qualify under the eligibility criteria, thereby securing the winning bid.

Beyond construction, I ensured integration with Haryana's digital warehouse receipt system, aligning with national commodity trading exchanges, and enabling real-time inventory stack updates and electronic pledge financing. The warehouse continues to operate successfully, serving a vital role in India's agricultural storage backbone.

### Key Deliverables

- Strategic project pitching to private investors
- Land procurement and regulatory clearances
- Tender drafting and legal negotiations
- Financial bid modeling and optimization
- Integration with digital warehouse receipt systems
- Setup of operational processes and vendor tie-ups

### Results

- 88,000 metric ton storage capacity commissioned
- Seamless handover to operational management
- Enabled digital tracking and commodity exchange integration
- Reduction in food-grain loss, imacting local population food safety
