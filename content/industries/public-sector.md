## Overview

Working at the intersection of governance, infrastructure, and strategy, my public sector engagements focused on developing robust economic and financial models that informed public-private partnerships (PPP) across emerging economies.

These projects demanded a unique mix of strategic foresight, multi-stakeholder alignment, and data-grounded financial modeling. Many of the engagements were executed under intense scrutiny with tight timelines, typically in collaboration with government bodies and consulting teams.

I contributed to strategy and execution support in key areas such as:

- **Power sector reform and subsidy design**
- **Investor Awareness**
- **PPP structuring in Agriculture & Horticulture**

## Key Focus Areas

- PPP project design & viability modeling
- Stakeholder consultations and policy briefs
- Regulatory impact assessment
- Public finance and budget structuring
- Value for money (VfM) analysis

> “The complexity of public infrastructure isn't just in the capital—it’s in creating value for every invested rupee.”
