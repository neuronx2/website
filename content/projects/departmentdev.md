## Project Goal

To establish a structured legal recovery framework for non-performing assets (NPAs) in the B2C credit card segment following the 2008 financial crisis. The objective was to build a data-informed, compliant recovery system that integrated legal processes and on-ground operations, targeting defaulting customers through both analytical and field strategies.

### Stack

- Excel + SQL for portfolio analysis and recovery tracking
- CRM systems for delinquency classification and field activity logging
- Legal frameworks: SARFAESI Act, RBI guidelines, DRT workflows
- Geo-targeting tools for legal recovery camps
- Audit-ready documentation and standardized notice templates

### Implementation Steps

- Designed SOPs for legal recoverability assessment, litigation procedures, and customer communication
- Segmented portfolio using amount overdue, age of delinquency, and repayment behavior
- Launched targeted recovery campaigns in high-NPA regions with legal and field teams
- Coordinated with external lawyers and collections agents for litigation and negotiation
- Tracked metrics including Days in Collection, Legal Action Success Rate, and Asset Recovery

### Results

- Reduced manual effort by 40% through account prioritization and template-driven communication
- Developed KPI's & dashboards for real-time NPA tracking and team performance
- Enabled compliant, replicable legal recovery model under RBI supervision

### Strategic Impact

The project institutionalized legal NPA recovery for B2C credit risk at GE Capital in India. It provided a scalable framework that combined compliance, analytics, and operations to systematically address high-risk accounts.
