## Project Goal

To optimize the use of discounts and vouchers in customer acquisition and retention by building a data-driven discount analytics framework. The project aimed to enhance the effectiveness and efficiency of discounting through structured testing, campaign insights, and scenario-based budget allocation.

### Stack

- SQL (Redshift) for campaign and order data extraction
- Python (Pandas, Statsmodels) for A/B testing and regression modeling
- Power BI / Tableau for dynamic dashboards
- Snowflake + dbt for pipeline transformation
- Amplitude + Segment for user behavior tracking
- Excel for budget simulations and sensitivity modeling

### Methodology

- Designed A/B and multivariate testing architecture to evaluate discount impact
- Segmented users by acquisition channel, region, and lifecycle stage
- Measured campaign KPIs including CPA, redemption, ROI, and LTV uplift
- Built a scenario model for allocating budget across voucher types and user cohorts
- Developed dashboards to monitor campaign performance, budget utilization, and test results in real time

### Results

- Discontinued low-performing voucher types
- Increased ROI on discount spend by 5%
- Achieved 8% uplift in conversion in high-performing segments
- Streamlined Quarterly Business Review preparation through real-time reporting dashboards

### Strategic Impact

This project established a robust discount optimization process that combined analytics, experimentation, and visualization. It became integral to quarterly marketing reviews, enabling finance and marketing to make aligned, data-backed decisions on voucher strategies and campaign planning.
