## Project Goal

To lead a full-scale digital transformation for a IT enabled marketing research company. The objective was to modernize fragmented data systems, integrate siloed departments, and lay down digital infrastructure for future scalability and efficeint reporting.

### Stack

- ERP implementation (GP Dynamics)
- Azure Data Lake for centralized data storage
- Power BI for enterprise dashboards
- Internal documentation repositories for governance and onboarding

### Implementation Steps

- Conducted enterprise audit to identify inefficiencies and outdated systems
- Consolidated manufacturing, finance, and customer data into centralized server
- Rolled out Power BI dashboards for real-time plant, finance, and procurement KPIs
- Delivered ERP integration roadmap with phased rollout by region and department
- Enabled data-driven decision making and digital reporting to board members

### Results

- Reduced reporting delays by 60%
- Unified global operations data into a single cloud source
- Created a digital foundation enabling predictive analytics and automation
- Increased visibility of supply chain and customer order tracking

### Strategic Impact

This project marked a cultural and operational shift in how a legacy enterprise approached data, technology, and strategy. The transformation laid the groundwork for cross-site collaboration, and faster decision-making at the executive level.
